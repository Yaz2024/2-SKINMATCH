## About the project

SkinMatch is a skincare website thought and designed to help people find a personalized routine that fits their skin and their daily needs. We wanted to create a simple and interactive experience for people who are interested in skincare like us, but also for people that are not always sure which products to choose based on their needs and budget.
The website combines a personalised skincare quiz with a product shop, ingredient informations and other interactive features.

## Target audience

Our main target audience is Gen Z and Millennials who are interested in skincare and want to discover products that fit their needs, preferences and pockets in the easier and faster way possible. (18/35 yrs)

## Main goal

Our goal is to make skincare easier, more personal and more relaxing. We want our users to explore the products while learnimg about different ingredients and finding the easier routine that works for them. We would also like to act as retailers for the products we recommend through the quiz, offering users immediate solutions and encouraging them to make a purchase directly on our website, rather than leaving the site to search for the recommended products on another sites.

## Main interactions

The main feature is the skincare quiz. Users select their skin type, budget and the amount of time they want to spend on their routine. Based on their answers, JavaScript displays a skincare profile and personalised suggestions.
The website also includes product category filters to help users discover the most suitable products for their needs, a shopping cart where users can add and remove products, an ingredient carousel and a newsletter form to join and be part of our SkinMatch family. Finally, we also included our contacts through wich the user can contact us thanks to the links that direct to our main communication channels.

## How JavaScript works

JavaScript makes the website interactive. It reads the user's answers in the quiz and uses conditions to show in the response different profiles and skincare suggestions. The product filters show only the selected category, while the shopping cart updates the number of items and the total price when products are added or removed. (The cart and the final price are only for showcasing purpose).
The newsletter form displays a confirmation message after a valid email is entered. The checkout is only a demo and does not ask or process real payments.

## Technologies used

## How to run the project

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Open the project folder with all the files in Visual Studio Code.
3. Open `index.html` in a browser, or install and use directly the Live Server extension.
4. Explore the website and try the quiz, product filters and shopping cart.
